Atelier Equipment

Precision Instruments & Tooling

We combine Swiss horological instruments with heavy-duty industrial machinery to achieve tolerances within thousandths of a millimeter.

Stereo Microscopes

40x magnification optics utilized for micro-soldering chip decoders and inspecting locomotive valve gear alignment.

Micro-Lathes

Custom turning of replacement drive axles, piston rods, and bespoke flywheel components with micron accuracy.

Ultrasonic Tanks

Multi-stage solvent and aqueous cleaning baths removing microscopic machining oils prior to painting and assembly.

DCC Test Benches

Specialized rolling roads and oscilloscope diagnostics calibrating motor load, back-EMF, and multi-channel sound synchrony.
