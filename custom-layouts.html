The Journey

Our Process

Much like charting a transcontinental railway, every commission follows a meticulous six-stage itinerary from initial spark to maiden voyage.

01

Consultation

We discuss your available space, preferred era, budget, and operational aspirations.

schedule 1-2 Hours
02

Concept & Scale

Selecting ideal scales (HO, OO, N, O) and drafting thematic narratives and track geometry.

architecture Blueprints
03

Layout Planning

3D CAD routing, gradient calculations, staging yards, and structural benchwork design.

view_in_ar 3D Modeling
04

Track & Electrical

Hand-laid or flex track installation, DCC bus wiring, decoders, and automated signaling.

bolt DCC Integration
05

Scenery & Structures

Terrain sculpting, plaster shells, custom weathering, vegetation, and scratch-built buildings.

landscape Hand Sculpted
06

Testing & Install

Rigorous rolling stock burn-in tests, fine-tuning, professional delivery, and white-glove setup.

local_shipping White-Glove
