Our Story

Building Trust, One Miniature at a Time

With decades of combined experience in craftsmanship, our atelier has built a reputation on precision, passion, and uncompromising quality in the world of miniature railways.

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded by passionate railway enthusiasts, Iron & Gauge Atelier began as a small workshop dedicated to repairing vintage models. Today, we stand as a premier destination for collectors seeking bespoke layouts and museum-grade restorations.

Every project we undertake is guided by a commitment to historical accuracy, aesthetic beauty, and mechanical perfection.
